# UrApex — Competitor Analysis

> Analysis of existing tools in the sim racing data/analytics space.
> The goal is to understand the landscape, not to copy.
> UrApex must be differentiated in concept, design, and identity.

---

## Analysis Framework

For each competitor, we evaluate:
- **Core focus** — what is it primarily for?
- **Strengths** — what does it do well?
- **Weaknesses** — what is missing or poor?
- **Audience** — who uses it?
- **Business model** — free / paid / freemium?
- **Differentiation opportunity** — what UrApex can do better or differently

---

## Category 1 — Sim-Specific Tools

### iRacing Statistics (built-in + iRacing.com)
- **Focus:** Race statistics and iRacing-specific performance
- **Strengths:** Deep integration with iRacing, official data, iRating/SR visible
- **Weaknesses:** iRacing-only, no multi-sim, basic analytics, no improvement tracking
- **Audience:** iRacing users
- **Model:** Included with iRacing subscription
- **UrApex opportunity:** Multi-sim coverage, driver improvement focus, better UX

---

### ACC Results Analyzer / MoTeC (ACC telemetry)
- **Focus:** Telemetry analysis for Assetto Corsa Competizione
- **Strengths:** Deep telemetry, professional-grade analysis
- **Weaknesses:** Complex UI, no web access, single-sim, no progression tracking
- **Audience:** Serious ACC sim racers, esports teams
- **Model:** Free (ACC-specific)
- **UrApex opportunity:** Simpler UX, web-based, progression tracking, multi-sim

---

## Category 2 — Multi-Sim / Generic Tools

### SimRacingGP / SimRacing-Apps
- **Focus:** Lap time tracking, leaderboards
- **Strengths:** Simple to use, some community features
- **Weaknesses:** Basic stats, poor UX, no progression tracking, no insights
- **Audience:** Casual sim racers
- **Model:** Free
- **UrApex opportunity:** Better analytics, improvement tracking, design quality

---

## Category 3 — Adjacent Tools

### Racepace App
- **Focus:** Race analysis and lap time tracking
- **Strengths:** Mobile-first, some community
- **Weaknesses:** Limited analytics, no multi-sim depth
- **UrApex opportunity:** Web-first with depth, driver scores, AI coach

---

### SimHub
- **Focus:** Dashboards, overlays, hardware integration
- **Strengths:** Extremely feature-rich, hardware support
- **Weaknesses:** Not a web app, no progression tracking, complex setup
- **Audience:** Power users, streamers
- **UrApex opportunity:** Web-accessible, no setup required, focused on improvement

---

## Category 4 — General Sports Analytics

### Strava (cycling/running)
- **Not a competitor, but a UX reference.**
- Strengths: habit loop, gamification, social features, progression tracking
- Weaknesses: not sim racing
- **UrApex lesson:** The "post-activity ritual" (Strava upload after every run) is what UrApex should emulate.

---

### Whoop (health performance)
- **Not a competitor, but a product model reference.**
- Strengths: proprietary scores, daily feedback, behavior change focus
- **UrApex lesson:** Driver scores (like Whoop's Recovery, Strain, Sleep scores) give users a vocabulary for their performance.

## Things to NOT Copy

- Do not copy names, branding, or visual identity from any competitor
- Do not copy sim manufacturer logos or branding
- Do not copy leaderboard structures that may be proprietary
- Do not reference other tools by name in the UI or marketing
